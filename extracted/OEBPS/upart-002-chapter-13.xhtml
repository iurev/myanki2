CHAPTER 13

Section 13.1

(audio 27:36)

— Tenho de ir até Lisboa. — diz a Marina.

“I have to go to Lisbon,” says Marina.

— Eu posso ajudar. Eu conheço bem Lisboa.

E sei onde é o museu de arqueologia. — diz o Ricardo.

“I can help. I know Lisbon well.

And I know where the archeology museum is,” says Ricardo.

A Marina pensa na proposta.

Marina thinks about the proposal.

— O que é aquilo? — pergunta a Marina a apontar para alguma coisa perto dos prédios.

“What is that?” asks Marina pointing to something near the buildings.

— Aquilo é um homem de barba.

“That’s a bearded man.”

— Eu acho que conheço aquele homem.

“I think that I know that man.”

— Ai, sim?

“Oh, really?”

— Sim, ele está sempre onde eu estou.

“Yes, he’s always where I am.”

— Hmm. Tens razão. Eu também acho que conheço aquela barba.

Mas deve ser coincidência. Vamos embora.

“Hmm. You’re right. I think I know that beard too.

But it must be a coincidence. Let’s go.”

* * *

NOTES

A Marina pensa na (1) proposta.

Marina thinks about the proposal.

(1)

pensar em: to think about

  • Eu penso nas férias. I think about my (the) vacation.

‎— O que é aquilo (2)? — pergunta a Marina a apontar para alguma coisa perto dos prédios.

“What is that?” asks Marina pointing to something near the buildings.

(2)

aquilo: that

This demonstrative is used when the thing you are referring to is far from the speaker and the person receiving the message. This is a generic demonstrative, used when you are not defining the noun in terms of gender and number. It’s common in the question, What is that (over there)? and in the answer, That is….

  • O que é aquilo? What is that (over there)?
  • Aquilo é um livro. That is a book.

— Eu acho (3) que conheço aquele (4) homem.

“I think that I know that man.”

(3)

achar (que): to think (that), to be of the opinion (that), to believe

  • Eu acho que este restaurante é bom. I think that this restaurant is good.

The difference between pensar and achar, is that pensar involves a mental process, and achar is used to express an opinion, likes and dislikes.

‎(4)

aquele: that (masculine, singular)

This demonstrative is used when the thing you are referring to is far from the speaker and the person receiving the message, and you are characterizing it in terms of gender and number. In this case, it refers to a masculine, singular noun.

  • Aquele livro é azul. That book is blue.

— Sim, ele está sempre (5) onde eu estou.

“Yes, he’s always where I am.”

(5)

You place sempre (always) after the verb to refer to a recurrent event.

  • Eu estou sempre cansado. I am always tired.

— Hmm. Tens razão. Eu também acho que conheço aquela (6) barba. Mas deve ser coincidência. Vamos embora (7).

“Hmm. You’re right. I think I know that beard too. But it must be a coincidence. Let’s go.”

(6)

aquela: that (feminine, singular)

This demonstrative is used when what you are referring to is far from the speaker and the person receiving the message, and you are characterizing it in terms of gender and number. In this case, it refers to a feminine, singular noun.

  • Aquela caneta é azul. That pen is blue.

So far, you’ve learned these demonstratives (este, esse, etc.) in the singular form, when you are referring to a singular noun. If you are referring to a plural noun, the demonstrative will also have a plural form. For that, you only need to add a final -s.

Summary of the demonstratives

generic:

  • isto (this)
  • isso (that, close to you)
  • aquilo (that, over there)

specific (this):

  • este (masculine, singular)
  • esta (feminine, singular)
  • estes (masculine, plural)
  • estas (feminine, plural)

specific (that, close to you):

  • esse (masculine, singular)
  • essa (feminine, singular)
  • esses (masculine, plural)
  • essas (feminine, plural)

specific (that, over there):

  • aquele (masculine, singular)
  • aquela (feminine, singular)
  • aqueles (masculine, plural)
  • aquelas (feminine, plural)

‎(7)

ir embora: to go away, to leave

  • Vocês já vão embora? Are you leaving already?

Section 13.2

(audio 28:51)

— Eu quero comprar uma garrafa de água. Está muito calor. — diz a Marina.

“I want to buy a bottle of water. It’s very hot,” says Marina.

— Vamos àquele café.

“Let’s go to that coffee shop.”

De repente, a Marina sente alguém a puxar a mala.

Suddenly, Marina feels someone pulling her (the) bag.

Ela olha para trás. É o homem de barba.

She looks back. It’s the bearded man.

Ele mete uma mão no pescoço dela e rouba o colar.

He puts a hand on her (the) neck and steals the necklace.

— Ladrão! — grita a Marina.

“Thief!” shouts Marina.

O Ricardo consegue agarrar no braço do homem de barba.

Ricardo manages to grab the bearded man’s arm.

Eles lutam na rua.

They fight on the street.

O Ricardo consegue agarrar no colar.

Ricardo manages to grab the necklace.

Depois, tenta agarrar no homem de barba, mas ele foge.

Then he tries to grab the bearded man, but he runs away.

A Marina ajuda o Ricardo a levantar-se.

Marina helps Ricardo to get up.

— Estás bem?

“Are you alright?”

— Sim. Obrigado. Aqui está o colar.

“Yes. Thank you. Here’s the necklace.”

Afinal, a Marina acha que o Ricardo é boa pessoa.

After all, Marina thinks Ricardo is a good person.

Ela decide aceitar a ajuda dele e ir a Lisboa.

She decides to accept his help and go to Lisbon.

* * *

NOTES

— Vamos àquele (1) café.

“Let’s go to that coffee shop.”

(1)

You will contract the preposition a with the following demonstratives:

  • a + aquela = àquela (to that)
  • a + aquele = àquele
  • a + aquelas = àquelas (to those)
  • a + aqueles = àquelas
  • Eu dou a informação àquelas pessoas. I give the information to those people. (dar a + aquelas pessoas = dar àquelas pessoas)

Ele mete uma mão no (2) pescoço dela e rouba o colar.

He puts a hand on her neck and steals the necklace.

(2)

meter em: to put in

  • Ele mete uma mão no bolso. He puts a hand in his (the) pocket. (no = em + o)

Depois, tenta agarrar no homem de barba, mas ele foge (3).

Then he tries to grab the bearded man, but he runs away.

(3)

The verb fugir (to run away, to escape) is an irregular verb in the present tense.

conjugation of the verb ‘fugir’ (present)

  • eu fujo (I escape)
  • tu foges (you escape)
  • ele foge (he escapes)
  • nós fugimos (we escape)
  • vocês fogem (you escape)
  • eles fogem (they escape)
  • Os criminosos fogem da prisão. The criminals escape from prison.

A Marina ajuda o Ricardo a levantar-se (4).

Marina helps Ricardo to get up.

(4)

ajudar (alguém) + a + infinitive: to help (someone) do something

  • Eu ajudo o Pedro a limpar a casa. I help Pedro clean the house.

Ela decide aceitar (5) a ajuda dele e ir a Lisboa.

She decides to accept his help and go to Lisbon.

(5)

decidir (to decide) + infinitive: to decide to do something

The structure decidir (conjugated) + infinitive of the main verb is used to say you decide to do something. The verb decidir is regular.

  • Eu decido ir para casa. I decide to go home.
