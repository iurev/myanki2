HOW TO WORK WITH THE BOOK

Welcome to the world of Portuguese stories! Learning Portuguese by reading stories is the best way to consolidate what you learn in a fun and memorable way.

This story is suitable for complete beginners of European Portuguese (Portugal). You will learn the basics of the language, including the structures and vocabulary you will need to communicate in simple everyday life situations.

The structure of the book

  • Overview of the language: a quick summary of the features of the Portuguese language.
  • Story with the translation: the story is divided into chapters. Each chapter includes the story with the English translation, notes explaining the new grammar topics and exercises to consolidate what you have learned.
  • Story in Portuguese: the complete story only in Portuguese so that you can review and practice without help.
  • Glossary of terms: a list of the words included in this book, divided by topic.
  • Grammar summaries: an overview of the most important grammar topics covered in the book.
  • Solutions: the key to the exercises.
  • Audio download: https://storyglot.com/audio

How to work with the book

1. Go to https://storyglot.com/audio to download the audio of the story.

2. Read the story. Follow each section of the story by reading the text in Portuguese, the translation, and the notes explaining the grammar topics.

3. Review and consolidate what you have learned with the recommended exercises.

4. Once you finish the story with the translation and the notes, you can read the story without translation to consolidate what you have learned and practice reading without help.

How to practice your listening comprehension

  1. Listen to the audio of the story without reading.
  2. Listen to the audio again and read the text simultaneously.
  3. Listen to the audio a third time without help.

Note:

You do not need to learn each section perfectly before moving on to the following section. The secret to language learning is in the reviewing, so read the book a second (and third!) time to consolidate everything. You will see how much you have learned every time you read it.

Also, you can listen to the story’s audio on the go, so listen to it every chance you get. You will get used to the sounds of the language and improve your listening comprehension in a natural way.
