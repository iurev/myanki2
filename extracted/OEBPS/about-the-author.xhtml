Portuguese Lab Academy

Prepare to feel at home in Portugal!

The Academy is an online platform where you can learn European Portuguese from the comfort of your home, from beginner to upper intermediate. In the Academy, you can follow a step-by-step program with video lessons, focusing on the different language skills, listening, speaking, writing, reading, grammar and pronunciation.
