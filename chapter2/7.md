**7. Phrases:**
*   `estar com fome` (to be hungry)
    *   **Example from the text:** `Tu estás com fome?` (Are you hungry?)
*   `olhar para` (to look at)
    *   **Example from the text:** `O João olha para o mar.` (João looks at the sea.)
*   `ver televisão` (to watch television)
*   `tomar o pequeno-almoço` (to have breakfast)
