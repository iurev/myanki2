CHAPTER 12

(audio 25:58)

O João cai na água.

João falls into the water.

A água está gelada e ele sente o frio na pele.

The water is freezing cold, and he feels the cold on his (the) skin.

O João tenta manter a cabeça fora de água.

João tries to keep his (the) head above water. (lit. outside of the water)

Ele sente alguma coisa a puxar a perna.

He feels something pulling on his (the) leg.

A cobra gigante está debaixo de água.

The giant snake is underwater.

Ele vê os olhos luminosos. Estão a olhar para ele.

He sees the bright eyes. They’re looking at him.

O João tenta sair do poço, mas não consegue.

João tries to get out of the well, but he can’t.

As paredes são muito altas.

The walls are very high.

A cobra puxa o João por um túnel e ele deixa de ver a superfície da água.

The snake pulls João through a tunnel, and he can no longer see the water’s surface. (lit. he stops seeing the water’s surface)

Eles saem para outra zona debaixo de água.

They exit to another area underwater.

O João tenta libertar-se da cobra e consegue.

João tries to free himself from the snake and succeeds (manages to).

De repente, um peixe cai dentro de água.

Suddenly, a fish falls into (inside) the water.

O peixe passa perto da cobra.

The fish goes by near the snake.

A cobra vai atrás do peixe.

The snake goes after the fish.

O João nada.

João swims.

NOTES

O João cai na (1) água.

João falls into the water.

(1)

cair em: to fall into.

The verb cair is irregular.

  • ‎O gato cai na água. The cat falls into the water.

conjugation of the verb ‘cair’ (present)

  • eu caio (I fall)
  • tu cais (you fall)
  • ele cai (he falls)
  • nós caímos (we fall)
  • vocês caem (you fall)
  • eles caem (they fall)

A cobra puxa o João por um túnel e ele deixa de ver (2) a superfície da água.

The snake pulls João through a tunnel, and he can no longer see (stops seeing) the water’s surface.

(2)

deixar (to let, to leave) + de + infinitive: to stop doing something, to no longer do something

The structure deixar (conjugated) + de + infinitive of the main verb is used to say that you stop doing something.

  • O Pedro deixa de fumar amanhã. Pedro will stop smoking tomorrow.

O João tenta libertar-se da (3) cobra, e consegue.

João tries to free himself from the snake and succeeds (manages to).

(3)

libertar-se de (reflexive verb): to break free from

  • O gato liberta-se da caixa. The cat frees itself from the box.
